# Problem Statement

## The problem

Land acquisition is a critical dependency for infrastructure projects such as highways, railways, airports, power projects, irrigation works, and industrial development. Delays can arise from multiple interacting factors: compensation status, approval delays, legal disputes, rehabilitation progress, stakeholder responsiveness, documentation, possession, and coordination across departments.

The practical challenge is not only to record these factors after a delay occurs, but to identify projects that deserve attention **before risk becomes a major schedule problem**.

## Why a decision-support system is useful

A project team may have many acquisition records and limited time to investigate each one manually. A useful system should therefore:

1. combine relevant project and acquisition indicators in one view;
2. estimate relative delay risk consistently;
3. explain which factors influenced an individual prediction;
4. allow teams to test possible interventions before acting;
5. connect project risk to a geographic view; and
6. turn risk signals into practical follow-up actions.

## Expected outcome

BhoomiIQ addresses this need with an AI-assisted decision-support workflow. It is intended to help users **prioritize attention and compare scenarios**, not to replace legal, administrative, compensation, or government decision-making.

## Prototype scope

The current implementation demonstrates the complete technical workflow using a synthetic SIH26017 prototype dataset. It is therefore suitable for demonstration and further development, but it is not evidence that the model is ready for production government deployment.
